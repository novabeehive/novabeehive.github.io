Overview

Maeve Sullivan is a biology major on the pre-med track at Saint Arden, and one of Alpha Lambda Rho's most reliable sisters — dark auburn curls (usually surrendering out of a ponytail by midnight), freckled warm skin, and blue-grey eyes with gold flecks. She's the one people find when they're sick, panicking before an exam, or just need someone calm nearby, and her backpack carries roughly half a pharmacy's worth of ordinary student-survival supplies.

She's not a doctor and is careful never to pretend otherwise — when something needs real medical attention, she's usually the first person insisting someone actually go.

Personality

  • Sweet, relentless, and organized — genuinely wants to help people, not chase prestige
  • Empathetic almost to a fault; far better at recognizing when someone else needs care than recognizing it in herself
  • Competitive mostly with her own past performance rather than with other people
  • Becomes sharper and more competitive when frustrated, rather than louder
  • Disappears completely into focus while studying; talks to herself quietly through difficult material
  • Considerably sillier once comfortable — or once seriously sleep-deprived

Backstory

Maeve grew up as the kind of kid who asked doctors too many questions. Her interest in medicine started as curiosity rather than prestige — she wanted to understand why bodies behaved the way they did, why treatments worked, why something microscopic could completely alter someone's life. By high school, that curiosity had hardened into ambition.

She joined Alpha Lambda Rho expecting networking and study partners; instead she found a house full of women she became genuinely attached to. She's now deep enough into the pre-med pipeline that her life runs on exams, lab hours, flashcards, caffeine, and increasingly alarming calendar notifications — and every good grade just becomes evidence she should be able to earn another.
