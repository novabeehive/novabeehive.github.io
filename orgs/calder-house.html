ABOUT THE HOUSE

Calder is where Saint Arden sticks students in studio art, graphic design, animation, photography, film, music production, fashion, illustration, and adjacent creative programs. An old converted residence hall near the arts district — exposed brick, enormous industrial windows, battered hardwood floors, weird staircases, basement practice rooms, communal studios, darkrooms, and walls painted over roughly four hundred times. Nobody's room looks normal. There's always music coming through a wall, and the laundry room has witnessed unspeakable things involving fabric dye.

Goth boys, emo boys, punk boys, metalheads, tattoo apprentices, art-school pretty boys, film nerds, musicians, photographers, skaters, pierced graphic-design disasters, fashion boys, sleep-deprived animators. A visually different pool entirely from ΦΚΑ and ΔΡΧ — frat boys show up polished or athletic, Calder residents show up looking like someone gave a Pinterest board student loans. There's a longstanding Calder-vs-Greek-Row rivalry, more bit than genuine: "nice pearls, Phi Kapp," followed three weeks later by that exact Calder boy waking up in the ΦΚΑ president's bed. Campus ecology. 🌱
