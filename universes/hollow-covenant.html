THE WORLD OF BRIARCOMBE

Hidden among mist-covered mountains and ancient forests, Briarcombe is a modern gothic settlement where humans and demons coexist under a centuries-old magical treaty known as the Briarcombe Concordat.

Four powerful demon houses protect the surrounding territories from supernatural threats. In exchange, a ceremonial lottery called The Drawing selects adult humans as prospective consorts. These magical marriages help sustain the protective wards — but a completed bond requires the conscious agreement of both parties.

A NOTE ON BONDS Marriage bonds do not automatically create love, obedience, or sexual consent.

FOR FELLOW CREATORS

The Hollow Covenant is a shared setting — its history, supernatural biology, political structure, marriage traditions, and magical rules are all built to be played in. Create human residents, demon nobles, household staff, investigators, merchants, scholars, supernatural beings, or entirely new stories within Briarcombe.

The four ruling houses and the Concordat form the established foundation. Explore new characters, personal histories, relationships, and conflicts — just keep the world's core rules consistent.

Individual house lorebooks carry additional character histories, estates, supporting casts, and private secrets.

Every house has its secrets.
Every promise has its price.
